Teaching point: for this idealized split-phase example with linear 120 V loads, the two leg currents are opposite in phase on the shared neutral. Therefore IN = |IL1 − IL2|. Equal L1 and L2 totals cancel at the service neutral even though current is still flowing in the branch circuits.

Try: make both sides 30 A total, then increase only one branch. Watch the white neutral waveform and service-neutral particle flow appear.
